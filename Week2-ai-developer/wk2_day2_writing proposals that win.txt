A Complete Proposal Example

Before you submit any proposal, read each sentence and ask: 
could my competition have written this sentence without reading this brief? If yes, 
cut it or rewrite it to be specific.



Brief-1: "I need a bot"
"Hello, I need someone to build me a bot that can reply to customer messages automatically. 
We sell agricultural inputs in Kenya and get a lot of WhatsApp messages asking about prices and stock. 
Budget is flexible. 
I need it ASAP."



Submiting proposal

Opening
You mentioned your team spends too much time answering repetitive WhatsApp messages about prices and stock. 
I can build an automated reply system that handles those common questions instantly, 24/7, so your team focuses only on conversations that actually need a human.

Understanding
Before building anything, I would want to understand three things: 
what platform you use for WhatsApp (WhatsApp Business app or the API), 
where your price and stock data currently lives, and 
whether you need the bot to take orders or just provide information.

Approach
I will set up an automated reply flow connected to your current product list. 
Customers asking about prices or availability get an instant, accurate response. 
Messages outside that scope get flagged for your team to handle manually.

Deliverables
A working automated reply system, a simple admin interface to update prices and stock, and
A 30-minute handover call so your team knows how to use and maintain it.

Price and timeline
KES 35,000. Ready in 10 working days from when you provide access to your WhatsApp Business account and product list. 
50% upfront, 50% on delivery.

Call to action
Happy to answer the three questions above before you decide. 
What platform do you currently use for WhatsApp?
