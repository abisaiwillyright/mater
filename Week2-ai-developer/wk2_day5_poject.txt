Project: The Full Client Cycle


Brief: Dairy Co-op Price Checker
"Hello, 
we run a small dairy co-op in Kiambu. We have about 30 farmer members who supply milk daily. 
Right now we have to call or WhatsApp each farmer to tell them the current buying price per litre, which changes weekly.
We want a simple tool where a farmer can enter their name and how many litres they supplied, and it will show them the total amount we owe them at today's price. 
We have no website. A simple program is fine. 
Budget: KES 8,000. Needed by end of week."



Stage 1: Read and Analyse the Brief
Problem         - Manual way of updating prices to farmers that need a automation.
The deliverable - Simple website or google doc that is attched with spreadsheep
The deadline    - 1 week
The Price       - KES 8,000
What is unclear:
what format does the farmer use to enter their name? 
How is the current price stored and updated? 
Who updates the price each week? 
Is this a command-line tool or something else? 
Does the tool need to store a history of payments or just calculate the current one?


Stage 2: Write a Clarifying Question
Suggested question: "Where does the current buying price live? 
If it is in a spreadsheet, I can read it directly. 
If someone types it in manually each week, I will need a simple way to update it."

Assumption in case they don't annswer to state in your proposal: 
"I am assuming the current price is stored in a simple text file or a single cell in a spreadsheet. 
If it is in a database or a more complex system, timeline extends by 2 day and this will affect the price as well."
