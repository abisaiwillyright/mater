Delivering Work Professionally

What You Hand Over at the End of a Project
1. A clean code - Well structured code.
2. A Readme - standard oparating procedure.
3. A delivery message - What you build
4. A written sign-off - Customer feedbck



Example 1 Delivery Message
Hello [Client],
The automated WhatsApp reply system is ready. Here is a summary of what was delivered:
- Replies automatically to messages asking about prices, stock, and delivery times
- Admin page at [URL] lets you update your product list and prices without touching code
- Messages outside the FAQ scope are flagged to your team with a daily digest
I have set up a test number you can use to try it: [number]. Send it any of the common questions your customers ask.
This covers everything in the original brief. Please review it over the next two business days. 
If everything looks good, send me a message confirming and I will invoice for the remaining 50%.
Let me know if you have questions.




Handling Revision Requests

Revision (included) 
"The bot is not replying to messages about delivery time."
Delivery time questions were in the agreed scope. If they are not working, that is your bug to fix at no extra charge.

Revision (Excluded)
"Can we add a feature where the bot also takes orders and updates our stock spreadsheet automatically?"
Order-taking was never in the brief. This is a separate project.
