Writing a Scope Statement
A scope statement has two sections: 
what is included and what is not included. 
The second section is as important as the first. 
Clients rarely know what is out of scope because they do not know what they do not know. You have to tell them.




Example Scope Statement: WhatsApp Bot Project

Included:
- Automated replies to questions about prices and stock availability
- Admin page to update the product list without code
- Daily digest of messages that required human follow-up
- 30-minute handover call

Not included:
- Order processing or payment collection
- Integration with any stock management software
- WhatsApp Business API account setup (client handles this)
- Changes to the product list format after handover
- Ongoing maintenance or updates after delivery

Communicate clearly to client to be on the same page.
Message - "Does this scope match what you need?"




Payment Structure: 50% Upfront, 50% on Delivery
Never start work without a deposit, It protects you if the client disappears.
It confirms the client is serious and gives you working capital.

For larger projects (over KES 100,000), break it into three: 
- 33% upfront, 
- 33% at a defined midpoint milestone, 
- 34% on delivery. This reduces risk on both sides.


send a payment request message
"Please send KES 17,500 (50% deposit) to [Paybill/Number]. 
This confirms we can start work. 
I will begin once the deposit is received."



Set Your Payment Terms Statement.

Payment Terms
50% deposit KES [amount] due before work begins.
Remaining 50% KES [amount] due within 2 business days of delivery confirmation.
Work does not start until the deposit is received.
Delivery timeline of [X] working days starts from the date the deposit clears.


Handling Late Payment
"Hello [Name], 
just following up on the balance of KES [amount] for the [project name] project. You confirmed delivery on [date]. 
If there are any issues with the work that need attention before you can pay, please let me know and I will address them. 
Otherwise, payment to [number/paybill] would be great. 
Thank you."


If there is no response after a second follow-up, sto sending reminders and focus on other things.
Chasing payment for weeks is not profitable regardless of the project rate.




Escrow on Upwork and Fiverr
This solves the payment problems.
The client deposits the project budget before you start. 
It sits with the platform. When you deliver and the client approves, the platform releases the money to you.





Today's Exercise
Write a complete project agreement for a sample engagement. Use this scenario: a small school wants a Python script that reads their student attendance Excel file and sends an automated SMS to parents when a student has been absent 3 or more days that week. Budget: KES 22,000. Timeline: 7 working days.

Your agreement must include:

Scope statement: what is included and what is not included (at least 3 items each)
Payment terms: deposit amount, final amount, when each is due
Timeline: start date (when deposit clears), delivery date
One sentence on what happens if the client requests changes after delivery that were not in scope
Keep it to one page. Plain language. No legal jargon. This should be something you can send on WhatsApp or email and the client immediately understands it.
