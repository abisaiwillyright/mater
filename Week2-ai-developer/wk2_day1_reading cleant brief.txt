Brief 1: "I need a bot"
"Hello, I need someone to build me a bot that can reply to customer messages automatically. 
We sell agricultural inputs in Kenya and get a lot of WhatsApp messages asking about prices and stock. 
Budget is flexible. 
I need it ASAP."


Working through Brief 1
Problem: Too many manual WhatsApp replies taking up staff time.
Deliverable: Some kind of automated reply system. ("Bot" is vague.)
Deadline: "ASAP" is not a deadline. This needs a specific date.

What is unclear: 
What platform is "WhatsApp"? 
The WhatsApp Business API? A third-party tool? 
What data does the bot pull prices from? A spreadsheet? A database? A manual list? 
Who maintains the price list? 
How many products are there? 
What happens when a question falls outside the bot's knowledge?




Brief 2: "Data dashboard"
"We want a dashboard showing our sales data. 
We currently use Excel and want something more visual. 
Our team is not technical. 
Budget is KES 25,000. 
Timeline: 2 weeks."


Working through Brief 2
Problem: Their Excel data is not visual or easy to share.
Deliverable: A dashboard. (Web-based? Printed report? Updated manually or automatically?)
Deadline: 2 weeks. Specific enough.

What is unclear: 
What is in the Excel file? 
How many rows? How often does it update? 
Do they want to update it themselves? 
What specific charts or metrics do they care about? 
Who will view the dashboard? 
On what device?




Brief 3: "Build me an app like Uber but for deliveries"
"I want to create a delivery app for Nairobi. 
It should work like Uber but for packages. 
Riders can sign up, businesses post deliveries, riders accept them. 
We want to launch in 3 months."


Working through Brief 3
Problem: No efficient local delivery platform with a two-sided marketplace.
Deliverable: A two-sided marketplace app (customer + rider apps, plus probably admin).
Deadline: 3 months. Probably not realistic for the scope described.

What is unclear: 
This is a 12-24 month project, not 3 months. 
What is the MVP? Do they understand the scope? 
Do they have funding? 
Are they looking for a no-code solution or a custom build? 
Have they done any market research? 
What payment integration do they want? 
This brief needs a serious scoping conversation before any proposal is written.
